## Executive finding

The original product thesis is not simply a generic visibility dashboard. Authority v1.0 states a Macau commercial multi-energy orchestration system that coordinates CEM grid supply, HVAC/chiller plants, thermal flexibility, PV, ESS, EV and hot water to minimize total economic energy cost subject to safety, comfort, equipment and business constraints. G7.2 then explicitly says economic dispatch should optimize value rather than maximize renewable use and lists grid import, PV usage, battery charge/discharge and HVAC operation as decision variables.

However, early commercialization/pilot documents sequence dispatch late: the G7.1 pilot plan places multi-energy dispatch in Phase 4 after visibility, optimization recommendations and limited control; G7.5's MVP roadmap puts PV/battery dispatch in Phase 5 after limited closed-loop control. This is a real product-sequencing tension against the earlier system thesis and the current product requirement that source/load dispatch be the primary user task. It does not prove the research direction was wrong. It means the business rollout plan, system objective and current product experience were not previously reconciled into one explicit, approved MVP narrative.

The current proposal in PR #10 resolves this as a **dispatch-first analytical workflow with evidence/readiness foundations and SHADOW-only recommendations**, without enabling device writes. It is still a draft; owner review is needed to decide whether this reflects the intended commercial MVP and how pilot phases should be sequenced.

## Package-by-package findings

### G7.1 — Market and ROI framework

**Package:** `macau-commercial-energy-os-g7.1-market-research-v0.1.zip` (5 entries): `README.md`, `CURRENT.md`, `MARKET_RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md`, `ROI_MODEL.md`, `PILOT_PLAN.md`.

- Lists target segments (integrated resorts, hotels, malls, office buildings, public facilities), pain points (energy cost, HVAC, peak demand, carbon reporting, complexity) and adoption factors (BMS, engineering maturity, security, procurement).
- Defines an ROI framework: energy-cost reduction, operations, maintenance and carbon-reporting value; historical baseline, optimized-operation comparison and M&V.
- Its pilot plan sequences Visibility → Optimization recommendation → Limited control → Multi-energy dispatch, with “prove measurable savings before automation.”
- `CURRENT.md` says customer segments, savings opportunity and commercial model are still being researched. The package contains a research framework, not market sizing, customer interviews, observed site load curves, pricing validation or measured ROI.

### G7.2 — Energy economics and dispatch

**Package:** `macau-commercial-energy-os-g7.2-energy-economics-v0.1.zip` (5 entries): `README.md`, `CURRENT.md`, `ENERGY_ECONOMICS_FRAMEWORK.md`, `MULTI_ENERGY_DISPATCH_MODEL.md`, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

- Objective explicitly favors economic value over renewable utilization alone: reduce cost and peak impact, preserve comfort/operations, and improve renewable use only where economically beneficial.
- Decision variables include grid import, PV use, battery charge/discharge and HVAC operation. The sketch includes grid, PV, battery, building load and HVAC. Dispatch considers tariffs, generation forecast, storage state and operational constraints; PV should not automatically replace grid electricity.
- The open-question file says Macau tariff details, typical hotel load curves, PV economics, battery payback, demand response and realistic savings percentages require further research.
- This is a useful conceptual frame consistent with source/load dispatch, but not a validated Macau tariff evaluator, reference-building dataset, feasible site schedule, battery business case or savings estimate.

### G7.5 Step 3 — Product platform blueprint

**Package:** `macau-commercial-energy-os-g7.5-step3-platform-blueprint-v0.1.zip` (7 entries).

- Defines Product, Platform, Intelligence and Edge planes; sketches Asset/Energy Graph, telemetry, forecasting, policy, constraints, optimization runs, command proposals and audit.
- Proposes separate identity, tenant, asset, telemetry, energy, optimizer, report and Edge services. The package does not provide proof that independently deployed microservices are necessary or implemented.
- ADR-065 (four-plane product architecture), ADR-066 (visibility/recommendation before autonomous control), ADR-067 (Asset/Energy Graph-centered domain model) are **Proposed**.
- Its MVP roadmap sequences Phase 1 visibility, Phase 2 tariff/anomaly, Phase 3 optimization recommendations, Phase 4 limited closed-loop control and Phase 5 PV/battery dispatch. This sequencing conflicts with a dispatch-first product experience and with “SHADOW before closed-loop” if interpreted as requiring control before dispatch. Resolve the interpretation explicitly; do not silently change it.

## Reconciliation with current main decisions and PR #10

Current main decisions provide a more specific basis for a dispatch-first **advisory assessment** than the early G7.1/G7.5 roadmaps:

- D-001/D-002: economic cost/value under constraints; not a kWh-only dashboard or renewable-maximization objective.
- D-003: HVAC/chiller is the first controllable-asset hypothesis; D-004: ESS is optional and site/economics-dependent.
- D-005/D-077/U-025: PV economic treatment and cross-site rights depend on contract, meter, CEM and legal evidence.
- D-006: Demand Guard may veto; D-055: initial exact economics is bounded to evidenced C1 active-energy + effective-dated TCA; D-056: deterministic tariff-aware supervisor before MPC; D-060: rebound is first-class; D-061: MPC has no privileged command path.
- D-009 includes the Energy Graph, tariff/contract and meter/settlement twins, HVAC model, Shadow and M&V.

PR #10 makes dispatch planning the explicit primary task and preserves evidence checks, optional/site-proven assets, physical-versus-settlement separation and SHADOW review. That is a **proposed product resolution** of the earlier task/roadmap gap, not proof that G7.1–G7.5 were wrong or that an owner approved a revised roadmap.

## Product/owner decision still required after evidence review

Decide whether the commercial MVP promise is:

1. **Dispatch-first advisory product**: users compare evidenced source/load schedules from the first release; onboarding/readiness/tariff work supports that task; all output stays SHADOW, with no field writes. (Current PR #10 proposal.)
2. **Phased foundation-first product**: first sell visibility/tariff and readiness, then introduce schedule comparison after the initial pilot evidence threshold. This matches parts of G7.1/G7.5 sequencing but requires explaining why the central economic dispatch job is deferred and defining the value of the first phase.

Both options preserve advisory-only MVP and evidence gates. They differ in product promise, first pilot acceptance criteria, prototype hierarchy, sales packaging and the sequence of domain implementation. No selection is made here.
