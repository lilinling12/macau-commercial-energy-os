# Energy Platform Trends

## Purpose

Research the evolution of commercial energy platforms.

## Direction

Energy platforms are moving from monitoring systems toward optimization systems.

Evolution:

Data collection

->

Visibility

->

Analytics

->

Optimization

->

Operational intelligence

## Energy OS Concept

Energy OS combines:

- device data
- cloud platform
- optimization capability
- human decision support

The goal is measurable operational value, not only dashboards.
