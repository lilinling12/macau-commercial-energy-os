## 1. Planning rules

- “Ready” means the task has enough semantics to be reviewed or implemented without guessing an unresolved owner decision. It does not mean it is approved.
- Stack-neutral tasks may define domain meaning, trust boundaries, acceptance behavior, path ownership and migration requirements. They must not introduce a production framework, wire format, generator, database schema or deployment topology.
- No task may infer site tariff, export rights, equipment controllability, savings, comfort feasibility or Macau site truth from synthetic fixtures.
- The MVP boundary remains read-only ingestion plus SHADOW assessment/review. No equipment command path is in this backlog.
- G7.9 Step 3 remains open until its source-linked exit criteria are reviewed and accepted.
