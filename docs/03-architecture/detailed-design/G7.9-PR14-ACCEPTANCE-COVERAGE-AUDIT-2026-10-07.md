## Additional implementation facts relevant to interpretation

- The assessment statuses are `VALIDATED_WITHIN_SCOPE`, `SCENARIO_ONLY`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED`, and `INFEASIBLE`; per-claim status is `ALLOWED` or `WITHHELD`, with a separate `ClaimScope`.
- Candidate generation uses a finite discrete action grid and bounded transition/search budget. Tests establish consistency within that declared space, not a general production optimum.
- Import-energy money is limited to exact interval matches under caller-supplied VERIFIED markers. The code does not authenticate the referenced account, contract, tariff or rates.
- Demand charge, export compensation, full bill, savings, comfort/service, controllability, cross-site credit and device control remain withheld in the dispatch assessment.
- The separate tariff-period and bill-replay unit tests do not upgrade the dispatch result into a customer bill or validate a site's Macau tariff applicability.
