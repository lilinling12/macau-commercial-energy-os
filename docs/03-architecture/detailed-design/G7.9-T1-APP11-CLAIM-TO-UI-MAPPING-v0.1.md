## 2. Semantic axes and presentation mapping

| Logical result dimension | Review vocabulary | UI treatment | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operation lifecycle | Accepted, queued, running, completed, failed/cancelled (proposal vocabulary) | Loading/progress/failure only when backed by an actual service lifecycle | A completed response does not mean a complete assessment |
| Physical assessment scope | Complete, partial, blocked, infeasible, scenario-only as applicable | Keep physical qualification beside the source/load schedule; retain its own scope and reason | A balanced synthetic profile is not site truth or service feasibility |
| Economic evaluation scope | Complete, partial, blocked, not calculated | Economic summary reports the evaluation status and the exact numerator/denominator or interval set | Do not label a partial result as a complete-period total |
| Claim disposition | `ALLOWED` or `WITHHELD` | Each claim gets its own disposition, independent from neighboring claims | `PARTIAL` is not a third claim disposition |
| Claim applicability scope | Exact settlement component, interval set, account/contract and revision as available | An allowed partial component names its covered intervals; excluded intervals and reason remain inspectable | Do not turn missing coverage into zero or widen a claim to the whole horizon |
| Evidence qualification | Measured, forecast, scenario, synthetic, assumed, stale, unresolved or policy-verified | Display provenance separately from readiness | An evidence label alone does not prove authorization or applicability |
| Resource service | Not assessed, pass, violation, unknown | Per-resource service status with its own evidence/reason | Electrical balance is not comfort, delivery or controllability |
| SHADOW review | Append-only human disposition in a future service | Distinct review panel tied to immutable result revision | Review is not execution authority |

### 2.1 Exact example represented by the UI state study

For a six-interval, synthetic presentation case:

- Economic evaluation: `PARTIAL`.
- Coverage: Macau time 09:00–13:00, four intervals covered; 13:00–15:00, two intervals uncovered.
- Grid-import energy-component claim: `ALLOWED` **only for the covered interval scope**. The study displays no currency amount because it is not an economic calculation.
- Full-horizon bill total: `WITHHELD`.
- Savings: `WITHHELD`.
- Demand charge: `WITHHELD`.
- Uncovered intervals are absent from the eligible component; they are not zero-valued.

This interval selection is authored solely to test presentation. It does not claim those four intervals had verified rates or correspond to a PR #14 optimizer result.

## 3. Logical view-model requirements

These are semantic requirements, not JSON property names:

1. Keep the assessment lifecycle, physical status, economic status, claim disposition, claim scope/coverage, evidence qualification, service result, and review disposition separately addressable.
2. Each claim references the result dimension, physical or settlement scope, input/evidence revision, applicable interval set, and stable withheld reason(s).
3. A partial economic component reports covered intervals and total eligible intervals. If the contract cannot return the exact covered set, do not display a scope-specific eligible component.
4. A monetary component, when actually calculated, carries its own currency, period, amount, rule/evaluator revision, and coverage. A partial component is never relabeled as a bill total or savings.
5. Full-bill, savings, export compensation, demand/Pu, controllability, comfort/service, and device-control claims are independently gated. One `ALLOWED` claim cannot promote another.
6. Missing, stale, ambiguous, assumed, and unauthorized inputs have distinct reason semantics; zero is a measured/calculated quantity, never a synonym for unknown or excluded.
7. Tenant/site authorization failure rejects the request before exposing any result. A display scope is not an authorization scope.
8. SHADOW review refers to an immutable result revision and cannot modify the assessment or authorize a device command.

| UI-CL-07 State-selector isolation | User selects the UI-only partial example | Fixture, schedule/chart and actual result remain unchanged; banner identifies the demonstration |
| UI-CL-08 Locale parity | Same result in Traditional Chinese, English and Portuguese draft | Status meaning, exact scope and withheld reasons remain equivalent; no language switches or hides a claim |
