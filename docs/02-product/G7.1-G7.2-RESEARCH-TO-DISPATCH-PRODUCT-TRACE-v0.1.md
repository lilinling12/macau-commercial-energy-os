## 1. What the early research actually establishes

### G7.1 — market and ROI research framework

Inspected local source package: `macau-commercial-energy-os-g7.1-market-research-v0.1.zip`, SHA-256 `F6B85F284547C3E22CFEB4A99ABE7C4D8A006E6AF08ABBE3F16C7BA56049F3BA`.

- `MARKET_RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` lists candidate segments: integrated resorts, hotels, malls, offices and public facilities. It names energy cost, HVAC efficiency, peak demand, carbon reporting and operations complexity as possible pain points; BMS, engineering maturity, security and procurement as adoption factors.
- `ROI_MODEL.md` defines a measurement frame—energy cost, operations, maintenance and carbon value; historical baseline versus optimized operation; M&V—but it supplies no Macau customer observation, approved savings rate, baseline dataset, price, willingness-to-pay evidence or ROI result.
- `CURRENT.md` says G7.1 research was still investigating customer segments, savings opportunity and commercial model at that snapshot. Treat the file as an early framework, not proof that market research or ROI validation was completed.

### G7.2 — energy economics and multi-energy dispatch research

Inspected local source package: `macau-commercial-energy-os-g7.2-energy-economics-v0.1.zip`, SHA-256 `38150F324E445317A1B8C203DBB5A77CBECC9543093FD0A4786D6BE1EF61748C`.

- `ENERGY_ECONOMICS_FRAMEWORK.md` makes economic value the objective rather than renewable utilization alone. Listed goals are reducing electricity cost and peak-demand impact, maintaining comfort/operating constraints, and improving renewable utilization when economic. Listed decisions are grid import, PV use, battery charge/discharge and HVAC operation.
- `MULTI_ENERGY_DISPATCH_MODEL.md` sketches grid/PV/battery/building-load/HVAC, and a forecast → optimization → safety validation → edge execution hierarchy. It explicitly rejects automatic “PV first” substitution and makes tariff, generation forecast, storage state and operating limits relevant.
- `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` lists Macau tariffs, hotel load curves, PV installation economics, battery payback, demand response and realistic savings percentage as further research. These are open in this source snapshot; later work must be cross-checked before treating any one as still open or closed.
- These documents contain a domain frame, variables and safety boundary; they do not contain a calibrated Macau building model, tariff/settlement engine, validated optimizer, measured savings or a complete schedule for EV/hot-water assets.

## 2. Trace into active repository decisions

The inspected GitHub main `docs/00-authority/decisions/DECISIONS.md` blob is `20df79d798b7c39f85778c70b8843fed94cf9428`. Its active decisions refine the early framework:

- D-001 positions the product as a Commercial Energy Operating System / orchestrator rather than a generic EMS dashboard.
- D-002 makes total economic energy cost/value—not minimum kWh—the optimization objective.
- D-003 prioritizes HVAC/chiller as the first controllable-asset hypothesis; D-004 makes ESS optional and site-economics-dependent.
- D-005 requires PV treatment to follow the customer's verified settlement contract; D-006 gives Demand Guard hard-veto authority.
- D-008 bars direct cloud writes to low-level plant controllers; D-009 names tariff/contract, meter/settlement, Energy Graph, HVAC model, SHADOW and M&V as the MVP foundation.
- D-010's initial C1/C2 hotel/large-commercial site profile is still a GTM hypothesis, not a committed pilot customer.

These are current repository decisions, but site-specific tariff/contract facts, operator needs and physical capability still require evidence. Public grid interconnection or a general tariff model does not prove another building's PV settlement rights, any site's controllability or a saving.

## 4. Product interpretation and remaining proof

The early G7.2 frame is correctly translated into a first-class dispatch workflow in the PR #8/PR #10 proposals. The major remaining gap is empirical and authority evidence, not a missing high-level flow:

1. Qualify a real site's meters, timestamp/quality, physical topology, tariffs, contracts, settlement accounts and billing intervals.
2. Establish baseline and forecast quality plus the actual capacity, efficiency, SOC, ramp/rebound, comfort/SLA and manual-override envelopes for each participating asset.
3. Validate interval balance and dispatch feasibility against replay/simulation and then authorized site evidence; distinguish physical energy flow from bill settlement at every stage.
4. Validate candidate user roles, decision cadence, accepted evidence and procurement/commercial model with Macau site/finance/operator participants.
5. Only compute bill-grade cost, PV export credit, cross-building allocation, savings or ROI when the corresponding tariff/account/customer evidence is available.

The current synthetic interface may illustrate grid import, PV self-use, ESS charging/discharging and HVAC shift/rebound. EV and hot-water are eligible product-model categories only when site-specific meter mapping, technical capacity, operating constraints and settlement authority are evidenced; the early G7.2 package did not validate them.
