# Visual Reference Case Studies — UI/UX Quality, Not Templates

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-05  
**Scope:** Reference evidence for Macau Commercial Energy OS product surfaces. These cases inform craft and evaluation questions; they do not approve a visual direction, establish product requirements, or prove usability for energy operators.

## How to use this sheet

For each reference, separate (1) facts verifiable from the award body or official case summary, (2) the design lesson inferred for this product, and (3) what must not be copied. Re-open the work before a design sprint because award status, site behavior, and platform guidance can change. The review below is a source-level desk review; it is not a hands-on usability test or a complete accessibility audit of the reference sites.

## Case studies

### Rijksmuseum Collection Online — Webby 2025, Best User Interface

**Verified:** The Webby Awards records both the Webby Winner and People's Voice Winner for Best User Interface in 2025. The project credits Fabrique & Q42. The award category describes a seamless end-user interface.  
**Inference for this product:** Treat complex operational information as something users need to find, compare, and understand, not as a wall of KPIs. In dispatch, provide clear object/site context, progressive detail, meaningful filtering, and a route from a result to its supporting evidence. Let dense analytical content remain structured rather than disguising it as decoration.  
**Do not copy:** Museum collection metaphors, visual composition, or browsing patterns. An operator needs bounded decisions, constraints, and provenance; collection discovery is a different task.  
**Sources:** [Webby winner record](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2025/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-user-interface/333807/rijksmuseum-collection-online); [the award category](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-user-interface).

### Google Store — Webby 2026, People's Voice Winner, Best User Interface

**Verified:** The Webby record identifies Google Store as the 2026 People's Voice Winner for Best User Interface and a nominee for Best Mobile User Experience.  
**Inference for this product:** Review whether responsive layouts preserve the same decision context across screen sizes, and whether comparisons make differences legible without forcing users to reconstruct them mentally. For dispatch scenario comparison, keep the time window, objective, constraints, source coverage, and uncertainty aligned across alternatives.  
**Do not copy:** Retail persuasion, product merchandising, or commerce-specific interaction patterns. Their goal is purchase choice; the operator's goal is evidence-backed operational review.  
**Sources:** [Webby winner record](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2026/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-user-interface/378591/google-store); [Best User Interface category](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-user-interface).

### Proof — Awwwards Site of the Day, 2022-10-14

**Verified:** Awwwards identifies Proof as Site of the Day, tags it with data visualization, 3D, gestures/interaction and interaction design, and describes it as an experiential rebrand connecting purpose and outcomes.  
**Inference for this product:** A distinctive product identity can connect visual expression to a clear narrative. Data visualization can be an intentional interaction surface when users can still understand the underlying values and context.  
**Do not copy:** 3D, unusual navigation, or gesture-led interaction into routine operations by default. The page is an experiential brand project, not evidence that those techniques improve monitoring, dispatch review, or accessibility.  
**Source:** [Awwwards case record](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/proof-1). The page was source-reviewed; the remote page did not fully load during this review.

### Ceramic Beats — FWA of the Day, 2026-09-22

**Verified:** The FWA's official RSS case summary says the project turns 144 real objects from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's open-access ceramics collection into a nine-track sequencer, with each tile representing an object and its material. The FWA case page itself returned no readable body in the research browser; the award/date and summary are verified from the official feed only.  
**Inference for this product:** A meaningful mapping between source data and interaction can make a complex data set explorable. For energy dispatch, preserve a truthful link between physical flow, interval values, selected schedule, and the evidence behind it; use interactive exploration only when it clarifies the decision.  
**Do not copy:** Music/sequencer metaphors or playful interaction into consequential energy review. A creative data transformation is not a model for settlement, control authority, or safety.  
**Source:** [FWA official case feed](https://thefwa.com/rss/), entry dated 2026-09-22.

### Every Neuron — FWA of the Day, 2026-09-20

**Verified:** The FWA's official RSS summary describes a browser explorer for 22,691 real cells in a fruit-fly neural wiring diagram. The case page itself returned no readable body in the research browser; this is a feed-summary review, not a hands-on inspection.  
**Inference for this product:** Large, relational systems can benefit from progressive exploration when users can retain context and understand what the visualization represents. An energy graph or schedule view should reveal relationships without hiding units, provenance, freshness, or uncertainty.  
**Do not copy:** Neurobiology imagery, scale, or immersive exploration patterns. Operational users need a fast path to site-specific exceptions and actionable evidence, not an unconstrained atlas.  
**Source:** [FWA official case feed](https://thefwa.com/rss/), entry dated 2026-09-20.

## Product-specific synthesis

For the **operator console**, prioritize legible comparison, stable context, traceable evidence, explicit unknowns, keyboard access, responsive reflow, and reversible review states. A visual signature may come from a coherent type system, semantic energy-flow encoding, restrained motion, and carefully composed explanation—not from a pile of cards or decorative 3D.

For a separate **marketing site**, narrative, editorial composition, and expressive motion may be explored more freely, provided performance, accessibility, reduced-motion, and truthful product claims remain intact. Marketing-site awards are not direct evidence for the operator console.

Evaluate any proposed reference-inspired design against one real task: verify source/contract readiness, compare dispatch schedules, understand cost and constraints, inspect evidence, and review a recommendation in Shadow mode. Record where each number came from and whether it is measured, forecast, estimated, or synthetic. Never imply device execution.

## Award and quality boundary

Webby, Awwwards, and FWA recognition demonstrates that a particular work received a stated recognition; it does not prove accessibility, task success, energy-domain suitability, or superiority for this product. Use award sources to discover craft and originality, then judge the Macau experience against its own task outcomes, trust, accessibility, localization, and operator feedback. Do not claim “award-winning quality” or “award-ready” as a completed validation result.
